Splice plan, in order

Start at the top. "Splice" is how many trees to splice (each one uses 1 seed of each ingredient), "Regrow" how many extra trees to plant from that item's own seeds. "Seeds back" is the expected number of that item's seeds after harvesting and, if ticked, breaking the blocks.

Seeds in this recipe tree: inventory, "buy instead", max blocks

Put in seeds you already own; they come off the totals. Tick Buy for any seed you'd rather buy from a vending machine; everything below it is then left out. Max blocks is the "Up to X" on the wiki page; 8 or more earns the green F and makes an item worth replanting, 4 means it is not farmable. 1 of N marks an item whose recipe is shared with others: that tree drops a random one of them.

Add or fix a recipe

Missing a newer item, or a recipe changed? Add it here. It's saved in this browser and overrides the built-in recipe with the same name.

Where the recipes come from. 1,095 splice recipes from community recipe lists that follow the Growtopia Wiki tier guides. Every rarity comes from the wiki's Items By Rarity list; the two items with ? for a rarity are spliceable but appear nowhere on that list, so their trees are counted as dropping no seed. Farmable tags and max blocks per tree come from the wiki's Farmability Guide. This now covers the wiki's whole Splicable category. 178 items share a recipe with another, because one tree drops a random one of the set; those are marked 1 of N. Anything still missing can be added with "Add or fix a recipe".
Why the numbers are big. An item's rarity is the sum of the base seeds in its recipe, so one Science Station (rarity 82) is built from 82 Dirt, Rock, Lava and Cave Background seeds even if every in-between tree gave back exactly one seed. Most in-between trees give back less than one, so real totals are higher.

How the estimate works. Max blocks per tree come from the wiki's "Up to N" counts (Grass 16; Bricks, Wooden and Rock Background 12; many blocks 8; most other items 4). Each tree drops on average 2.5 blocks for an "up to 4" tree, 3.75 for up to 8, 5 for up to 12 and 6.25 for up to 16. A tree also drops its own seed with a chance that gets lower as rarity goes up: about 1 in 3 at rarity 1–3, 1 in 4 at 4–7, 1 in 5 at 8–11, and so on, falling by one step every 4 rarity. A broken block has a flat 25% seed chance and a 10% chance to give the block back, which works out to about 1 seed per 3.6 blocks. These are community averages from the Growtopia forums and wiki, not exact game code, and real results vary with luck; that's what the safety margin is for. Harvester, Deep Sorrow, roles and other buffs aren't counted, so with buffs you'll usually need fewer seeds than shown.
